## Before reporting a problem

Confirm the plugin version, host (ChatGPT or Codex), operating environment, whether the skill was explicitly invoked, and whether the conversation had a clear `ON` state. Reproduce the issue in a new conversation with synthetic, non-sensitive content when possible.

## Public support

Use GitHub Issues at:

<https://github.com/battle-doll/premilume/issues>

Include:

- plugin and host version;
- language and exact synthetic prompt;
- expected behavior;
- observed behavior;
- minimal reproduction steps.

Do not include credentials, private conversations, customer or employer data, personal identifiers, proprietary files, or screenshots containing sensitive information.

## Security reports

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability or privacy incident that could expose data or enable harmful actions. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use the private vulnerability reporting channel enabled for the public repository. If that channel becomes unavailable, send no sensitive details; open only a minimal issue asking the maintainer to restore a private channel.

## Support scope

Maintainers may help with installation, activation, routing, documentation, and reproducible package defects. They cannot provide emergency response, professional medical/legal/financial advice, account recovery, or support for unrelated host and third-party service outages.
